Lingaraj Temple

Bhubaneswar, Odisha

The Lingaraj Temple in Odisha is one of the oldest temples in India, dating back to the 11th century. It is known for its Kalinga architecture and the self-oriented Shiva Lingam inside the temple, which is believed to have appeared on its own. The temple stands as a magnificent example of ancient Indian architectural prowess and spiritual significance.

How to Reach

  • Nearest Airport: Bhubaneswar Airport (5 km)
  • Nearest Railway: Bhubaneswar Railway Station (3 km)
  • Local Transport: Auto-rickshaws and city buses available

Best Time to Visit

  • October to March
  • Early morning visits recommended
  • Avoid summer months (April-June)

Aarti Timings

  • Morning Aarti: 6:00 AM
  • Afternoon Aarti: 1:00 PM
  • Evening Aarti: 8:00 PM
💫 Fun Fact
The temple complex has 50 smaller shrines and is a major pilgrimage site for Hindus.
