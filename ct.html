Chandramouleshwar Temple

Near Hubli, Karnataka

The Chandramouleshwar Temple in, near Hubli, Karnataka, is an ancient temple dedicated to Lord Shiva. According to local legends, it was built overnight by spirits under the command of the Pandavas during their exile. The temple is renowned for its beautiful architecture, featuring intricate carvings and a serene atmosphere.

How to Reach

  • Nearest Airport: Hubli Airport (30 km)
  • Nearest Railway: Hubli Junction (25 km)
  • Local Transport: Local buses and taxis available
  • Morning hours preferred
  • Avoid peak summer (March-May)

    • Aarti Timings

    • Morning Aarti: 6:30 AM
    • Noon Aarti: 12:30 PM
    • Evening Aarti: 7:30 PM

    Best Time to Visit

    • September to February
    💫 Fun Fact
    The temple is believed to have been built in a single night by the Pandavas during their exile.
