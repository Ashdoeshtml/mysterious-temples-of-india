Vishnupad Temple

Gaya, Bihar

The Vishnupad Temple is a Hindu temple dedicated to Lord Vishnu, located in Gaya, Bihar. The temple is marked by a footprint of Lord Vishnu, known as Dharmasila, which is imprinted in a basalt rock. The temple is believed to have been built in the 18th century by Queen Ahilyabai Holkar of Indore. It is a significant pilgrimage site for Hindus, especially for performing the Pind Daan ritual, which is believed to bring salvation to the souls of ancestors. The architecture of the temple is a fine example of the Nagara style, with intricate carvings and a towering spire. The temple complex also houses several smaller shrines dedicated to various deities.

How to Reach

  • Nearest Airport: Gaya International Airport (7 km)
  • Nearest Railway: Gaya Junction Railway Station (3 km)
  • Local Transport: Auto-rickshaws and taxis readily available

Best Time to Visit

  • October to March (Winter Season)
  • Avoid summer months (April-June)
  • Monsoon season (July-September) sees moderate crowds

Aarti Timings

  • Morning Aarti: 5:30 AM - 6:00 AM
  • Afternoon Aarti: 12:00 PM - 12:30 PM
  • Evening Aarti: 7:00 PM - 7:30 PM
💫 Fun Fact
During the Pitru Paksha (a 16-day period dedicated to ancestors), over 500,000 pilgrims visit the temple to perform Pind Daan rituals. The temple's sacred water tank, called Mukti Kund, is believed to have been created by Lord Vishnu himself using his mace (Gada).
