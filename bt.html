Betal Temple

Amona, Goa

The Betal Temple in Amona, Goa, is dedicated to the warrior god Betal, who is often associated with ghosts. The temple is surrounded by eerie tales of spirits, and it is believed to possess mystical powers. The unique structure of the temple and its significant role in local folklore and rituals add to its mysterious allure.

How to Reach

  • Nearest Airport: Dabolim Airport, Goa (40 km)
  • Nearest Railway: Thivim Railway Station (15 km)
  • Local Transport: Buses and taxis from Panjim

Best Time to Visit

  • November to February
  • Early morning or evening preferred
  • Avoid monsoon season (June-September)

Aarti Timings

  • Morning Aarti: 6:00 AM
  • Evening Aarti: 6:30 PM
  • Special Puja: Full Moon nights
💫 Fun Fact
The temple is known for its unique rituals and is a significant part of local folklore.
