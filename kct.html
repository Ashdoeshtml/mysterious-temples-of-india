Kedareshwar Cave Temple

Harishchandragad Fort, Maharashtra

Located in the majestic Harishchandragad fort in Maharashtra, this temple is renowned for its mysterious one-pillar structure. At its heart stands a magnificent five-foot Shiva Lingam, supported by a single pillar, while the remnants of three fallen pillars tell a tale of time gone by. According to ancient local legends, these four pillars symbolize the four Yugas (cosmic ages), and it's believed that the fall of the final standing pillar would herald the end of our world as we know it.

How to Reach

  • Nearest Airport: Pune Airport (120 km)
  • Nearest Railway: Igatpuri Railway Station (45 km)
  • Local Transport: Jeeps available from Khireshwar village

Best Time to Visit

  • October to February
  • Early morning trek recommended
  • Avoid monsoon season (June-September)

Aarti Timings

  • Morning Aarti: Sunrise
  • Evening Aarti: Sunset
  • Note: Timings may vary by season
💫 Fun Fact
The temple is located inside a cave, which adds to its mysterious and adventurous appeal.
