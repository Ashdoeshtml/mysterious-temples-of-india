Somnath Temple

Veraval, Gujarat

Located in Veraval, the Somnath Temple is one of the twelve Jyotirlinga shrines dedicated to Lord Shiva. The temple has been destroyed and rebuilt many times throughout history. It is believed that the Shivling inside the temple has mystical powers and can produce gold by itself.

How to Reach

  • Nearest Airport: Diu Airport (65 km)
  • Nearest Railway: Veraval Railway Station (5 km)
  • Local Transport: Taxis and buses available from Veraval

Best Time to Visit

  • November to February (Winter Season)
  • Early mornings and evenings recommended
  • Avoid monsoon season (July-September)

Aarti Timings

  • Morning Aarti: 7:00 AM
  • Afternoon Aarti: 12:00 PM
  • Evening Aarti: 7:00 PM
💫 Fun Fact
The temple is said to have been built by the Moon God, Soma, in gold, then rebuilt by Ravana in silver, by Krishna in wood, and by Bhimdev in stone.
